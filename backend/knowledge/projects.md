## Lurnix — AI-Powered Learning Management System

### Technologies
- Node.js
- Express.js
- React.js
- MongoDB
- Gemini API
- Stripe Payment Gateway

### Overview
A full-stack Learning Management System (LMS) that enables educators to create and sell courses while students can purchase and access learning content.

### Features and Contributions
- Built a full-stack LMS for educators and students.
- Developed secure REST APIs with authentication, protected routes, and role-based authorization.
- Integrated AI-powered features, including an AI Quiz Generator, Roadmap Builder, and AI resume checker using the Gemini API.
- **AI Quiz Generator** - Users can generate personalized quizzes to test their knowledge based on specific topics. Before generating the quiz, they can:
  1. Enter the topic or subjects they want to be tested on (e.g., Java, React.js, Data Structures & Algorithms).
  2. Select the number of questions (5, 10, 15, or 20).
  3. Choose the difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard).
- Based on the provided inputs, AI generates a quiz with relevant multiple-choice questions. After submitting the quiz, users receive their results, including their score and performance. All quiz results are automatically stored in the history section, allowing users to review their previous attempts and track their learning progress.
- **AI Roadmap Generator** - Users can generate a personalized learning roadmap based on their goals and preferences. Before generating the roadmap, they can:
  1. Enter their learning goal or the technology/skill they want to learn (e.g., Android Development, MERN Stack, Data Structures & Algorithms).
  2. Select their current skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
  3. Choose their available daily study time (e.g., 1 hour/day, 2 hours/day, 3 hours/day).
  4. Select their preferred learning style (Visual, Practical, Theoretical, or Mixed).
- Based on the provided inputs, AI generates a structured, step-by-step learning roadmap with relevant topics, milestones, and learning resources to help users achieve their goals efficiently. they can also download that generated roadmap in pdf format
- **AI Resume Analyzer** - Users can analyze their resumes using AI to evaluate their profile and measure how well it matches a target job description (JD). Before generating the analysis, they can:
  1. Upload their resume in PDF format or paste their resume text directly.
  2. Optionally provide the target job description (JD) to evaluate their resume against specific job requirements.
- Based on the provided inputs, AI analyzes the resume and generates a detailed report, including an overall resume score, JD match percentage, keyword alignment, matching skills, missing skills, and areas for improvement. Users can identify gaps between their profile and the job requirements and receive suggestions to improve their resume and increase their chances of getting shortlisted.
- Built community discussion modules that enable students and educators to share questions, comments, and learning resources.
- Implemented course enrollment and payment workflows using the Stripe Payment Gateway.
- Designed backend services using Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB.
- Deployed the application using cloud platforms.
